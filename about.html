Our Story

Rooted in Awka, Built on Trust

Ochie Realty began with a simple observation: finding reliable property information in Awka was harder than it needed to be. Listings were scattered, details were inconsistent, and buyers and tenants often had to visit multiple agents before finding something that matched what they were told.

We set out to change that — building a single, trustworthy place where people can search, compare and understand properties across Awka and its surrounding communities before they ever step outside their door.
